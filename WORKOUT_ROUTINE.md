## Loads and progression

Home dumbbells currently stop at 15 lb each; planned adjustable capacity is approximately 5–50 lb each. Equipment capacity and prior logged weights are not starting-load prescriptions. Use the gym's racks, safeties, barbells, bench, pull-up bar, and platform for the heavy sessions. Use a stable bench or sofa for Friday hip thrusts.

Add load only after every prescribed working set reaches the top of its repetition range with clean form and about three reps still available. Reduce load or volume when recovery or form is poor, and deload when needed. The tracker does not execute Liftosaur's adaptive scripts or either inspiration program's full progression system. Any later change to prescribed sets or reps must be another immutable Sheet version.

## Running and time constraints

Runs remain Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, and Sunday mornings; Sunday is the longest/hardest. Monday's lower-body work follows that run, so preserve the three-repetition reserve and lower the load when Sunday recovery is incomplete. The preferred gym window is 12:15–1:15 PM; a one-hour fit has not yet been established. No extra strength day is added.
