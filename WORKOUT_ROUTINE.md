# Actual workout routine

This document describes the active Google Sheet routine snapshot `ws4sb-4day-v1`, effective September 10, 2026. The Google Sheet remains the source of truth.

The program follows a six-week cycle with four strength sessions each week. Monday and Wednesday are one-hour gym sessions. Friday and Sunday are home sessions using only 5–15 kg dumbbells and bodyweight. The main upper- and lower-body gym lifts rotate every two weeks; the remaining exercises stay consistent throughout the cycle.

## Monday — Gym upper and legs

Begin with the rotating upper-body strength lift. In weeks 1–2, work up to one heavy set of 3–5 bench-press reps after warming up. In weeks 3–4, use the incline bench press for the same prescription. In weeks 5–6, use the close-grip bench press.

Continue with three sets of 8–12 Bulgarian split squats. Then perform three sets of 8–12 seated cable rows and three sets of 12–15 face pulls as a superset. Finish with three sets of 10–15 leg curls.

Rest three minutes after the heavy press and about 90 seconds after the other exercises or completed superset rounds.

## Wednesday — Gym lower and upper

Start with five sets of three jump squats, focusing on maximum height and a clean landing. Follow with the rotating lower-body strength lift. In weeks 1–2, perform three working sets of 3–5 box squats after warming up. In weeks 3–4, work up to one heavy set of 3–5 deadlift reps. In weeks 5–6, work up to one heavy set of 3–5 squat reps.

Continue with three sets of 10–15 dumbbell bench presses. Then perform three sets of 8–12 lat pulldowns and three sets of 12–15 reverse flyes as a superset. Finish with three sets of 10–15 V-ups.

Rest three minutes after the heavy lower-body lift, two minutes after dumbbell bench presses, 90 seconds after jump squats and completed pulling superset rounds, and 60 seconds after V-ups.

## Friday — Home upper

Perform four sets of 10–20 dumbbell floor presses together with four sets of 10–20 one-arm dumbbell rows per side as a superset. Lower each floor-press rep for three seconds, and pause at the top of every row.

Next, perform three sets of 10–15 dumbbell shoulder presses together with three sets of 15–25 strict dumbbell lateral raises as a superset. Finish with three sets of 12–20 hammer curls together with three sets of 12–20 overhead dumbbell triceps extensions as a superset.

Rest about 75 seconds after each press-and-row round and 60 seconds after each shoulder or arm superset round.

## Sunday — Home lower

Perform four sets of 12–20 goblet squats, lowering for three seconds and pausing at the bottom. Continue with four sets of 12–20 dumbbell Romanian deadlifts using a slow lowering phase and a full hamstring stretch. Then perform three sets of 10–15 reverse lunges per side, four sets of 15–25 single-leg calf raises per side, and three sets of 10–15 V-ups. Pause at the top and stretch at the bottom of each calf raise.

Rest about 75 seconds after squats, Romanian deadlifts, and lunges, and 60 seconds after calf raises and V-ups.

## Progression

Use the performed workouts to establish working weights. For home exercises, first add controlled reps until reaching the top of the prescribed range. Then move to the next available dumbbell while keeping clean technique. When 15 kg is no longer challenging, increase difficulty with slower lowering, longer pauses, greater range of motion, or unilateral execution instead of exceeding the available equipment.
